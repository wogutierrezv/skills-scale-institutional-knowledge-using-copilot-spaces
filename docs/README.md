# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes the project management process guidance used by OctoAcme to help teams initiate, plan, execute, communicate, release, and improve work consistently. These documents provide a lightweight but practical framework for managing cross-functional projects from concept to completion.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle that starts with project initiation, where teams define the problem, identify stakeholders, set success metrics, and create a one-pager to align on scope and goals. Once a project is approved, the team moves into planning, turning the initiative into a backlog, milestone plan, risk register, and clear definition of done. During execution, the team uses standing rituals such as standups, delivery syncs, reviews, and governance checkpoints to manage progress, dependencies, and blockers. Quality is maintained through clear acceptance criteria, CI checks, testing, and release readiness gates.

Roles are intentionally defined to support accountability and cross-functional collaboration. Product managers define what matters most and track outcomes; project managers coordinate plan execution, dependencies, communication, and risk management; developers build and validate delivery; and QA partners confirm quality and acceptance criteria. Communication is handled through recurring updates, stakeholder reporting, and escalation paths designed to keep work visible while ensuring that major issues reach the right decision-makers quickly.

The team also emphasizes risk management and operational readiness. Risks are logged, assessed, and monitored through a standard process, while communication templates provide a consistent way to share progress, blockers, and incident impact. Releasing features follows a defined checklist that includes staging validation, smoke tests, rollback planning, stakeholder communication, and post-deploy verification. This keeps delivery disciplined without slowing down the team.

To close the project cycle, OctoAcme uses retrospectives and continuous improvement practices to capture lessons learned, assign action items, and turn feedback into tangible improvements. The goal is to create a sustainable, transparent, and repeatable project management model that supports team alignment, quality, and measurable value delivery.
